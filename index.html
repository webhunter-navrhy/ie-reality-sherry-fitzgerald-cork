About the property

Description property

Welcome to 73 Leslie's Arch, a well presented four-bedroom, four-bathroom townhouse extending over three floors and situated within the highly regarded Old Quarter development, one of Ballincollig's most desirable residential addresses.

Beautifully maintained and finished to an excellent standard, this impressive home is enhanced by contemporary décor and a warm, inviting colour palette throughout. Boasting a highly efficient B3 Energy Rating, the property also qualifies for attractive green mortgage rates.

Extending to approximately 1,722 sq. ft., this spacious home has been thoughtfully designed to suit modern family living.

Upon entering, a welcoming entrance hall leads to a beautiful kitchen, newly fitted in 2023, which seamlessly connects to the dining and living areas, creating the perfect space for everyday living and entertaining. A utility room and guest bathroom complete the ground floor accommodation.

The first floor comprises of the master bedroom, complete with an en-suite bathroom and the family room spans the full width of the property. Featuring an elegant bay window and French doors opening onto a Juliet balcony, this space is flooded with natural light. The second floor comprises three further bedrooms, one of which benefits from an en-suite, together with a well-appointed family bathroom.

Outside, the front of the property features a low-maintenance garden with an attractive cobblelock finished and enclosed by cast iron railings, along with a designated parking space. To the rear, the fully enclosed garden is laid with lawn and a patio area, providing an ideal setting for outdoor entertaining and al fresco dining.

The location of this home is second to none, with Ballincollig town centre just a short stroll away, offering an excellent selection of shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday amenities. A range of highly regarded primary and secondary schools are within easy reach, while the beautiful Regional Park is within distance of the property.

Excellent transport links further enhance the appeal of this outstanding home, with a convenient 24-hour bus service nearby and direct access to the N40, providing swift connections to Bishopstown, MTU, Cork University Hospital and Cork City Centre.

Offering generous accommodation, modern finishes and an unrivalled location, 73 Leslie's Arch presents a great opportunity to acquire a wonderful family home in one of Ballincollig's most sought-after developments.
